## Clearance rule

Before a Nyx asset is reused on a new page, record all of the following in
this file:

1. Direct source-page URL, not only a search result or collection page.
2. Asset-specific licence and required credit line.
3. Whether the licence permits repository redistribution and derivative WebP
   optimisation.
4. The local file hash after optimisation.

The Star Citizen Wiki publishes content under CC BY-SA unless otherwise noted,
but that general statement is not enough to establish that a local binary came
from a particular page or to determine an image's individual credit. Official
CIG pages can establish current location status; they are not image-reuse
permission.

## First page after clearance

The first new Nyx destination should be `src/pages/nyx/levski/index.mdx`.
Levski is a confirmed, playable landing zone in Nyx; it needs a Levski- or
Delamar-specific cleared hero image and a direct source citation before the
page is added. Do not substitute Nyx I–III or generic station pages merely to
fill the route map.
