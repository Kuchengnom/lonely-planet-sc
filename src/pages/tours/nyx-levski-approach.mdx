## Ship & Cargo Notes

- **Primary: Caterpillar (L)** — maximum hold for trade or salvage runs; slow to dock.
- **Alternative: Constellation Andromeda (M)** — faster approach, lighter crew, fewer pallets.
- Bring a **handheld scanner** and **repair kit**; the fringe is not forgiving.
- If you are staging salvage, confirm your hold layout before departure.
