## Ship & Cargo Notes

- **Primary: Cutlass Black (M)** — balanced cargo hold, decent firepower for pirate or animal encounters, easy to land on ridge shelves.
- **Alternative: Freelancer MAX (S)** — faster, smaller footprint for tight ice shelves, lower cargo ceiling.
- Pack **sample crates** and **camera gear**; keep **Oxygen** and a **repair kit** in the hold, not the suit.
- If you plan a detour for live specimens, add a medical box and secure tie-downs.
