## Ship & Cargo Notes

- **Primary: Caterpillar (L)** — massive cargo capacity for sample returns, robust shielding against thermal and radiation exposure, redundant systems for emergency scenarios. The Caterpillar's modular design allows you to configure the hold for mineral samples, salvaged parts, or crystal collections.
- **Alternative: Constellation Andromeda (L)** — excellent sensor suite for seismological monitoring, strong defensive capabilities for claim disputes, and comfortable crew quarters for the 6-hour surface survey.
- Pack **sample crates** and **extraction tools**; keep **emergency beacon** and **seismometer** in the hold, not the suit.
- If you plan to collect irradiated pearls from valakkars, add a secured containment box and radiation shielding. The pearls have unusual regenerative properties that researchers are eager to study.
- **Cargo capacity note**: A fully loaded Caterpillar can return with 48 sample crates. At current Pyro I market rates (fluctuating), that's 240,000–360,000 UEC gross before expenses.
