## Ship & Cargo Notes

- **Primary: Cutlass Black (M)** — forgiving landing in industrial zones; decent cargo for samples and spares; decent firepower for unexpected encounters.
- **Alternative: Freelancer MAX (S)** — better approach speed, lighter hold, easier to find landing space on crowded pads.
- Mask and water are non-optional; the air is technically breathable but practically unpleasant.
- UEC for drinks at M&V's and coffee at the platform kiosk.
- If you plan to capture photos at sunset, bring a polarizing filter — the haze creates spectacular light.
