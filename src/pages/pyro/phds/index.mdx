# Project Hidden Discovery Sites — Pyro System

The Pyro system is 18 light years from civilization — close enough to reach, far enough to die in. Its planets are geological nightmares: active volcanism, crushing gravity, atmospheres that would dissolve a standard suit in minutes. Yet even here, amid the chaos, the community has discovered places of strange beauty and deeper mystery.

This database entry follows the same standards as the [Stanton PHDS](/lonely-planet-sc/stanton/phds/): every location is documented with coordinates, navigation instructions, required gear, and community sources. No unverified rumors. No placeholders.

## Research Principles

PHDS follows strict documentation standards:

- **No unverified rumors** — every entry requires coordinates or reliable navigation method
- **Cross-source validation** — at least two independent community reports
- **Safety information** — required gear and difficulty ratings are mandatory
- **Photographic value** — sites must have visual interest or narrative mystery

## Historical Context

Project Hidden Discovery Sites (PHDS) began as a community initiative on RSI Spectrum in 2024. What started as a simple Google Doc of shared coordinates evolved into a structured database following Lonely Planet's editorial standards.

The Pyro system entries reflect the system's particular challenges: extreme environments, limited infrastructure, and a population that survives by being self-reliant. Every entry in the Pyro section is a testament to the pilots who have braved these worlds and returned to share what they found.
