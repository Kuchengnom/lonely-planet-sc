# Project Hidden Discovery Sites — Pyro System

The Pyro system is 18 light years from civilization — close enough to reach, far enough to die in. Its planets are geological nightmares: active volcanism, crushing gravity, atmospheres that would dissolve a standard suit in minutes. Yet even here, amid the chaos, the community has discovered places of strange beauty and deeper mystery.

This database entry follows the same standards as the [Stanton PHDS](/lonely-planet-sc/stanton/phds/): every location is documented with coordinates, navigation instructions, required gear, and community sources. No unverified rumors. No placeholders.

## Current Database

### Pyro System Entries

**Bloom Abandoned Mining Sites** — Bloom (Pyro III)
- *System*: Pyro → Bloom (Pyro III) — multiple surface locations
- *Type*: Abandoned site
- *Difficulty*: **Moderate to Extreme** — EVA suit, water supply, scanner for mineral deposits
- *Status*: Fully documented
- *Why visit*: The ruins of Pyrotechnic Amalgamated's mining empire — silent, decaying, slowly being reclaimed by Bloom's frozen soil. Prospect Depot, Bueno Ravine, and Carver's Ridge each offer unique industrial decay and frontier history.

**Ruin Station Orbital Observation Post** — Terminus (Pyro VI)
- *System*: Pyro → Terminus (Pyro VI) — orbit, Lagrange point
- *Type*: Observation point
- *Difficulty*: **Extreme** — outlaw territory, XenoThreat-controlled
- *Status*: Fully documented
- *Why visit*: A sprawling, ugly, fascinating mess of hulls and modules bolted together over five centuries. The observation points offer a vantage point no other place in the verse can match — looking down at star Pyro from the system's outer reaches.

**Pyro I Distortion Storm Edge** — Pyro I
- *System*: Pyro → Pyro I — Lagrangian Point observations (L1–L5)
- *Type*: Natural wonder
- *Difficulty*: **Extreme** — proximity to star, distortion damage, irradiation
- *Status*: Fully documented
- *Why visit*: Two violent distortion storms churning in a planet's atmosphere at temperatures that shouldn't support atmospheric physics, surrounded by irradiation levels that would kill an unprotected human in minutes. From the L-point stations, it is apocalyptic, awe-inspiring, and lethal.

**Sacren's Plot** — Pyro IV
- *System*: Pyro → Pyro IV — northern temperate highlands, canyon outpost
- *Type*: Settlement (outpost)
- *Difficulty*: **Hard** — private property, Citizens for Prosperity garrison, turrets and missiles on the approach
- *Status*: Fully documented
- *Why visit*: A fortified outpost cut into a red rock canyon, held by the Citizens for Prosperity — the frontier organisation working to end Pyro's outlaw trade. The gate guards are not hostile, but they are thorough; inside the wire there is a working market, a trade terminal, and one of the few places on this planet where a stranger can still do business.

**The Frozen City** — Pyro V
- *System*: Pyro → Pyro V (Polar Plateau)
- *Type*: Mystery
- *Difficulty*: **Extreme** — thermal survival suit, emergency beacon, never go alone
- *Status*: Fully documented
- *Why visit*: A city carved entirely from ice, with no known origin, no records of construction, and mathematical patterns carved into its central sculpture. The most mysterious structure in the Stanton system, and perhaps the entire quadrant.

**Pyro I Crashed Vanguard Wreck** — Pyro I
- *System*: Pyro → Pyro I (Equator Canyon)
- *Type*: Easter-egg
- *Difficulty*: **Moderate** — quantum drive Nav3, landing gear B7 or larger
- *Status*: Fully documented
- *Why visit*: A Constellation Vanguard wreck embedded in Pyro I's equatorial canyon, surrounded by the kind of volcanic landscape that makes you appreciate your thermal suits.

## Research Principles

PHDS follows strict documentation standards:

- **No unverified rumors** — every entry requires coordinates or reliable navigation method
- **Cross-source validation** — at least two independent community reports
- **Safety information** — required gear and difficulty ratings are mandatory
- **Photographic value** — sites must have visual interest or narrative mystery

## Historical Context

Project Hidden Discovery Sites (PHDS) began as a community initiative on RSI Spectrum in 2024. What started as a simple Google Doc of shared coordinates evolved into a structured database following Lonely Planet's editorial standards.

The Pyro system entries reflect the system's particular challenges: extreme environments, limited infrastructure, and a population that survives by being self-reliant. Every entry in the Pyro section is a testament to the pilots who have braved these worlds and returned to share what they found.
