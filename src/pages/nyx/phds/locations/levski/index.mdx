## Introduction

Levski is the reason to visit Nyx. The People's Alliance turned an abandoned mining facility inside Delamar—the largest asteroid in the Glaciem Ring—into a settlement unlike any polished corporate landing zone. Its rough tunnels, reused industrial infrastructure, and political history are not background dressing; together they are the attraction.

**Levski is Nyx's only confirmed visitor destination.** The People's Alliance welcomes tourists in public areas, but residential checkpoints remain a real boundary. Respect the rules, and the place opens up as one of Star Citizen's strongest pieces of environmental storytelling.

## What to Bring

Delamar orbits within the Glaciem Ring, where asteroid fields are dense and the local environment remains harsh even in protected orbital interiors. Standard spacesuit protection is sufficient for surface and orbital areas, though thermal regulation helps when moving between sunlit and shadowed sections of the asteroid.

**Essential gear:**

- **Standard Spacesuit** — Levski's internal atmosphere is breathable, but external maintenance tunnels and surface access points may have variable composition. The suit's primary function is pressure containment.
- **Navigation Assistant** — Delamar's interior spans multiple levels and sectors. The orbital entrance leads to the Grand Barter area, but side corridors branch into residential and restricted zones.
- **Holo-camera or imaging device** — The lighting conditions inside Delamar create dramatic contrasts between industrial fixtures, exposed rock, and the People's Alliance's own installations. The Anthony Tanaka memorial especially benefits from careful framing.

## The Levski Circuit

**Make time for the Levski circuit** to understand the settlement's character:

**Grand Barter.** Start with the improvised market that best captures the settlement's communal, make-do character. The Grand Barter operates from the former mining supply bay, with stalls set up along the original rail system. Expect a mix of local production and transitory trade.

**Conscientious Objects and Cordry's.** Browse Levski's anti-establishment weapons, armour, clothing, and EVA culture. Exact stock changes with each build cycle as the People's Alliance rotates inventory through their distribution network.

**Café Musain.** Pause at the dark local meeting place for a drink and a quieter read on Levski life. The Café Musain operates in a former crew quarters section, with lighting deliberately subdued to encourage conversation.

**Anthony Tanaka memorial.** Finish at the hand-carved statue and its layers of protest graffiti. CIG's own visitor guide calls it the place to understand Levski's mix of idealism, anger, and independence. The memorial sits in what was once the central hydroponics bay, now converted to a public space.

<TouristTip>

- **Residential boundaries:** The residential zones are marked but not enforced by visible checkpoints. Take cues from local signage and other visitors; if you're unsure whether you should be somewhere, you probably shouldn't be there.
- **Market timing:** The Grand Barter operates continuously, but specialized vendors rotate in and out based on supply cycles. Plan your visit if specific items are important to your itinerary.
- **Photography:** The interior lighting creates natural contrasts between warm industrial fixtures and cool rock surfaces. The Anthony Tanaka memorial especially benefits from close-up shots that capture the layering of original carvings and subsequent protest graffiti.

## Getting There

From Delamar's orbital entrance, follow the main access corridors toward the settlement core. The Grand Barter area is the first major public space you'll encounter, with signs indicating the route to Café Musain and the Anthony Tanaka memorial.

**From orbital entry to Grand Barter:** 300–400 meters along the main corridor, with multiple side branches that eventually loop back to the market area.

**From Grand Barter to Anthony Tanaka memorial:** Approximately 1.2 kilometers through residential and commercial zones. The route passes through the former mining control center, now a public information hub.

## What You'll Find

Levski occupies what was once the primary mining facility on Delamar. The People's Alliance has repurposed the infrastructure without concealing its original function:

- **Grand Barter area** — Former mining supply storage, now a crowded market with stalls along the original rail lines
- **Conscientious Objects and Cordry's** — Located in what were crew quarters, with reinforced walls and modified lighting
- **Café Musain** — A former dining hall with low lighting and acoustic treatments added for conversation
- **Anthony Tanaka memorial** — The central hydroponics bay, with the statue carved directly into the rock face of Delamar's interior

The architecture mixes the asteroid's natural rock formations with industrial lighting, reused machinery, and People's Alliance signage. The overall effect is one of deliberate roughness—no attempt to hide the building's origins as a mining operation.

- **Café Musain interior** — Low-light shots that capture the atmospheric quality of the meeting space

## Safety Considerations

- **Jurisdiction:** Nyx is unclaimed territory, so there is no UEE customs or standard law enforcement. The People's Alliance maintains security around Levski.
- **Residential zones:** Stay in public areas unless invited. Residential checkpoints are not always visible, and approaching them may result in being asked to leave.
- **Communication:** Levski's internal comms network has limited range. For extended exploration, keep your ship's long-range systems active.
