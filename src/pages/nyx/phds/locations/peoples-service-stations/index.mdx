## Introduction

Four People's Service Stations (Alpha, Delta, Theta, and Lambda) operate in the Nyx system, offering restocking, rearming, refuelling, and shopping. They function as a safe haven in a system where safe havens are scarce—especially important given the current piracy reports and increasing Vanduul activity near the Virgil-Nyx jump point.

## What to Bring

Standard spacesuit protection is sufficient for station operations. The stations maintain climate-controlled interiors and regular UEE-compatible docking procedures.

**Essential gear:**

- **Fuel Cells or Quantum Travel Capable Ship** — All four stations offer refuelling services. If your ship requires specific fuel types, verify availability before arrival.
- **Standard Docking Procedure Knowledge** — The People's Alliance follows UEE docking protocols. Your ship's navigation computer should handle most procedures automatically.
- **UEC for shopping** — The stations carry a range of goods beyond essential services. Pricing is competitive with standard UEE markets.

## Access Notes

**System:** Nyx  \n
**Planets:** System-wide (stations orbit Delamar at different quadrants)  \n
**Jurisdiction:** People's Alliance (unclaimed territory)  \n
**Docking availability:** All four stations accept visitors, though Alpha is the primary entry point for first-time arrivals.

**Getting there:**

1. **Enter the Nyx system** via any jump point
2. **Navigate to Delamar** in the Glaciem Ring
3. **Choose your station** based on your approach vector:
   - **Alpha** — Primary orbital entrance, most complete services
   - **Delta** — Eastern quadrant, good for east-bound traffic
   - **Theta** — Southern approach, less crowded during peak times
   - **Lambda** — Western corridor, secondary option when primary stations are busy
4. **Follow standard docking procedures** — The People's Alliance uses UEE-compatible protocols

**Best time to visit:** All stations operate 24/7, but traffic varies. Alpha tends to be busiest during standard UEE business hours (08:00–18:00 local station time).

<TouristTip>

- **Station Alpha** is the primary visitor reception point. If this is your first visit to Nyx, start here for orientation and any questions about the settlement.
- **Shopping varies by station** — While all stations carry essential supplies, Alpha has the widest selection of goods due to its role as the primary reception point.
- **Security:** The People's Alliance maintains security at all stations. While the Nyx system has reported piracy incidents, the stations themselves are well-protected.

</TouristTip>

## What You'll Find

Each station provides the same core services:

- **Restocking** — Food, water, oxygen, and emergency supplies
- **Rearming** — Weapon refills and ammunition for standard UEE-compatible systems
- **Refuelling** — A306 and other standard fuel types
- **Shopping** — A range of goods from travel essentials to local produce

The stations are modular constructions attached to Delamar's orbital infrastructure. Interior layouts follow UEE design standards, with clear signage and station personnel stationed at key intersections.
