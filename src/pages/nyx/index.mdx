# Project Hidden Discovery Sites (PHDS)

Nyx's confirmed visitor destinations are documented as PHDS entries:

- [**Levski — The People's Alliance Settlement**](/phds/locations/levski/) — The settlement carved into Delamar, featuring the Grand Barter, Conscientious Objects, Café Musain, and the Anthony Tanaka memorial.
- [**People's Service Stations**](/phds/locations/peoples-service-stations/) — Alpha, Delta, Theta, and Lambda stations offering restocking, rearming, refuelling, and shopping in the standard spacelanes.

*These PHDS entries provide detailed navigation, required gear, and photographic opportunities for each location.*

<AttractionEntry number={1} title="Levski — Nyx's Essential Stop" category="settlement" image="/images/nyx/glaciem.webp" imageAlt="The Glaciem Ring around Delamar, home to the Levski settlement">

Levski is the reason to visit Nyx. The People's Alliance turned an abandoned mining facility inside Delamar — the largest asteroid in the Glaciem Ring — into a settlement unlike any polished corporate landing zone. Its rough tunnels, reused industrial infrastructure and political history are not background dressing; together they are the attraction.

**Make time for the Levski circuit:**

**Grand Barter.** Start with the improvised market that best captures the settlement's communal, make-do character.

**Conscientious Objects and Cordry's.** Browse Levski's anti-establishment weapons, armour, clothing and EVA culture; exact stock changes with the build.

**Café Musain.** Pause at the dark local meeting place for a drink and a quieter read on Levski life.

**Anthony Tanaka memorial.** Finish at the hand-carved statue and its layers of protest graffiti. CIG's own visitor guide calls it the place to understand Levski's mix of idealism, anger and independence.

Visitors are welcome in the public areas, but the residential checkpoints are a real boundary. Respect the People's Alliance rules and the place opens up as one of Star Citizen's strongest pieces of environmental storytelling.

</AttractionEntry>

<AttractionEntry number={2} title="The Keeger Belt — The Cold Edge" category="natural-wonder" image="/images/nyx/keeger.webp" imageAlt="The Keeger Belt at the outer edge of the Nyx system" >

Nyx's second belt sits at the very edge of the system, cold and distant, and is suspected of hiding criminal strongholds. People's Service Stations and QV Breaker Stations have been added here since Alpha 4.7. If you want to see what an unclaimed frontier looks like once somebody starts bolting infrastructure to it, this is the place.

</AttractionEntry>

<AttractionEntry number={3} title="The Worlds Beyond the Current Route" category="natural-wonder" image="/images/nyx/in-space.webp" imageAlt="The Nyx star glowing against a red nebula" >

Nyx I, Nyx II, and Nyx III explain the system's harsh reputation: a stripped coreless world, an acid-cloud planet, and an ice giant. They are useful orientation on the map, but this guide does not yet present them as visitable stops. Start with Delamar and the confirmed station network; expand the itinerary only as each destination has a reliable current route and source.

</AttractionEntry>

<AttractionEntry number={4} title="People's Service Stations — A Safe Harbour" category="practical">

Four stations (Alpha, Delta, Theta and Lambda) are run by the People's Alliance and offer restocking, rearming, refuelling and a surprising amount of shopping. It is a safe haven in a system where safe havens are scarce. Mind your manners: you are a guest of the people who live here.

</AttractionEntry>

<TouristTip>

- **Entry**: Nyx is unclaimed, so there is no UEE customs. The People's Alliance keeps the order around Levski.
- **Safety**: Stay on the standard spacelanes. Piracy is small-scale but real, and the cargo moving through is valuable.
- **Levski**: Visit the Grand Barter, Café Musain and the Anthony Tanaka memorial; keep the residential areas off the itinerary.
- **Resupply**: People's Service Stations (Alpha, Delta, Theta, Lambda) handle fuel, repairs and restocking.
- **Current patch caution**: Alpha 4.10.2 lists a trap near the Levski ASOP-terminal railings and a group-hangar bug. Do not jump the railings, and leave a friend's hangar before its owner does.
- **Status**: Nyx is playable, but this guide keeps its itinerary to Delamar/Levski and the confirmed service-station network. It does not present Nyx I–III as visitable stops until their current availability is independently verified.

</TouristTip>
