Terminus (also called Pyro VI) is the outermost planet in the Pyro system — a frozen, methane-laced world orbiting 68.3 gigameters from its host star. Daytime temperatures dip below -120°C. The surface is barren ice and permafrost, the atmosphere a poisonous soup of methane gas. It is, by every metric, one of the most hostile environments in the Stanton system.

And yet there, half-buried in millennia of frozen waste, sits a **Constellation Phoenix** — a 61-meter luxury touring vessel, one of the most expensive ships in the RSI lineup. The phoenix emblem on its hull peeks through the ice like a signature scrawled by a mad artist.

This is one of Star Citizen's great unanswered questions. No combat damage is visible. The evacuation hatch was opened from the inside, but the crew's emergency egress pods are gone — swept away by ice shifts or taken by scavengers. There are no bodies. No distress beacon. Just a gleaming luxury liner, reduced to a frozen sculpture on a world where nothing survives.

The wreck lies on the frozen plains below **Ruin Station**, the dilapidated space station that orbits Terminus. Ruin was originally MacEwan Station, built by Pyrotechnic Amalgamated in 2512. When mining profits failed, the company abandoned it to squatters and outlaws. Today it's run by the XenoThreat pack — a place where survival depends on who has the most guns. The station's lights are faintly visible from the crash site on clear days, a ghostly reminder that someone still lives in this wasteland.

## Getting There

Reaching Terminus is an adventure in itself. There are no commercial landings, no docking ports, no help. You arrive by quantum travel, then descend through a methane atmosphere into temperatures that would freeze a human being in seconds.

**From the Pyro System Hub:**

1. Quantum travel to **Terminus** from Ruin Station or the system hub.
2. Drop out of quantum just outside the atmosphere. Terminus appears as a pale, featureless sphere — a snowball with a poisonous atmosphere hanging around it like a shroud.
3. Enter the atmosphere carefully. Methane storms create severe turbulence. Watch your altimeter and thermal readings.
4. Navigate toward the frozen surface below. The crash site sits on the flat ice plains near Ruin Station's orbital ground track. The Phoenix's dark hull contrasts sharply with the white ice — it is easily spotted once you are within visual range.
5. Land your ship at a safe distance and approach on foot. The wreck is accessible from the surface but the environment is lethally hostile.

<TouristTip>
  Do not attempt to refuel or resupply at Ruin Station unless you are prepared for a fight. The XenoThreat pack that runs it is notorious for their violence, and tourism is not what they were built for. Buy what you need before entering the Pyro system.
</TouristTip>

**Navigation Tip:** The crash site is easiest to find from the surface — look for the unmistakable shape of a Phoenix's fuselage against the ice. From orbit, it is nearly invisible. Community guides suggest using Ruin Station as a rough reference point: the wreck lies on the ground track beneath the station's orbit.

## What You'll Find

### The Wreck

The Constellation Phoenix is RSI's flagship luxury transport — a large (S4) vessel with a crew capacity of 3-4, 80 SCU of cargo space, and amenities that include a master suite with panoramic windows, guest quarters, an entertainment deck, and a hidden sensor-dampened compartment for high-value cargo. It comes equipped with a Lynx rover and a Kruger P-72 Archimedes fighter. This was a ship designed for the ultra-wealthy to tour the galaxy in comfort.

Finding it smashed into Terminus's ice is like discovering a yacht buried in the Bermuda Triangle.

The fuselage is remarkably intact — a testament to the Phoenix's robust construction. The iconic phoenix symbol is visible through the frost, the hull windows are largely unbroken, and the overall structure remains recognizable. The engines retain a faint glow even in the freezing dark, their thermal signature a warm spot in the -120°C environment.

The interior reveals the ghost of luxury abandoned mid-journey: plush seating arrangements, personal effects scattered across the deck, crystal glassware still sitting on tables as if the owners stepped out for a moment and never came back. The master suite's panoramic window faces the ice ceiling — the last thing its passengers saw, presumably, before the end.

### The Landscape

Terminus is a world of ice and silence. The frozen surface stretches in every direction, cracked and wind-sculpted, with nothing but methane fog on the horizon. The crash site is isolated — hundreds of kilometers from the nearest significant terrain feature. The silence is absolute, broken only by the wind moving across the ice and the occasional groan of shifting permafrost.

Ruin Station hangs overhead on clear days, its lights a distant, indifferent glow. You can see it as a small, persistent star in the daytime sky — a reminder that life, of a sort, persists in this system, just not on the surface.

### The Mystery

Why did a luxury liner end up here? There are no theories that fully explain it. Some say navigation failure in a methane storm. Others suggest deliberate abandonment — the Phoenix's hidden compartment might have contained something so valuable that its owners chose destruction over delivery. The absence of bodies (or escape pods) only deepens the mystery.

Community pilots who visit the site report an uncanny feeling of watching something mid-evacuation, as if time itself froze when the ship hit the ice. There is no grave, no marker, no explanation. Just a ship and a question, preserved in permafrost.

## Photographic Opportunities

### Wide-Angle Landscape Shots

The stark contrast between the dark Phoenix hull and Terminus's white ice creates naturally dramatic compositions. Wide-angle shots capturing the wreck in its desolate context — ice plains stretching to a methane fog horizon — emphasize both the isolation and the mystery.

### Detail Photography

Close-ups of the phoenix emblem, the frost-crusted cockpit windows, and the interior details (plush seating, crystal glassware, the master suite window) tell the story of luxury interrupted. The contrast between opulent design and brutal environment is the visual heart of this location.

### Atmospheric Lighting

Methane storms create unique lighting conditions — diffuse, blue-gray, and otherworldly. Long exposures during storms capture the swirling gas, while the Phoenix's faint engine glow provides an unexpected warm accent against the cold palette. The station's distant lights, visible from the surface, can serve as background elements in night shots.

### Night Astrophotography

With minimal light pollution and Terminus's slow rotation, the night sky offers exceptional conditions for astrophotography. Position the wreck against the starfield for compositions that emphasize the vastness of the system and the ship's isolation within it.

## Safety Considerations

- **Lethal Cold**: Daytime temperatures of -120°C mean unprotected exposure is instant death. A thermal-resistant EVA suit rated for cryogenic conditions is mandatory. Do not underestimate the cold — Terminus does not forgive mistakes.
- **Methane Atmosphere**: The atmosphere is toxic, flammable, and offers zero oxygen. Atmospheric breathing equipment is non-negotiable. The XenoThreat packs who operate Ruin Station deal in methane processing — they understand what this gas can do.
- **Methane Storms**: These can reduce visibility to near zero and create severe turbulence for any surface activity. If a storm approaches, find shelter immediately. White-out conditions on featureless ice are a death sentence.
- **Radiation**: Despite being far from Pyro's star, cosmic radiation remains a concern. Ensure your suit's radiation shielding is adequate for extended surface exposure.
- **Structural Instability**: Years of ice pressure have warped the wreck. Do not climb on the hull or venture beyond the open hatch — the structure could collapse without warning.
- **Ruin Station Warning**: The outlaw pack operating the station above is dangerous and unpredictable. Do not attract attention, and under no circumstances attempt to land at Ruin Station for supplies.
